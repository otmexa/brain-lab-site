Research Focus

Scientific tone, clearer menus, and room for serious content.

The goal is not to imitate overloaded lab sites. The goal is to communicate neuroscience work with a stronger visual identity and much better readability.

  • Navigation should be immediate, not buried in visual clutter.
  • The front page should feel scientific without turning into a color explosion.
  • Every public section should support readable summaries and supporting material.
