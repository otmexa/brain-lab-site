Why this site exists

A research site that can grow with the project.

Instead of treating the website as a static brochure, the goal is to create a living surface for research communication: papers, people, datasets, internal notes turned public, and reusable supporting material.

Publishing model

Structured content, not manual edits for every update.

The public pages are prepared to load published records from Supabase. That means the owner can update content, upload files, and expand sections without turning every small content change into a full code task.
